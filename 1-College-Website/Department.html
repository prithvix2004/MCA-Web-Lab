Mohandas College of Engineering and Technology

Department of Computer Applications

Innovate. Code. Lead the Digital Future.

The Department of Computer Applications at Mohandas College of Engineering and Technology (MCET) offers a comprehensive four-year Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) programme affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU), Kerala. The department is dedicated to building a strong foundation in computer hardware, software design, and theoretical computing.

With an emphasis on OBE (Outcome Based Education), practical exposure, and industry-oriented skills, the curriculum covers modern paradigms including Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Cloud Computing, and Cybersecurity. Our mission is to transform students into competent software engineers and innovators capable of solving real-world problems.
