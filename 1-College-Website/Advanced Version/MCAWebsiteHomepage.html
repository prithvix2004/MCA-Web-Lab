Eligibility

Candidates who have completed a Bachelor's degree in the relevant discipline from a recognized university and satisfy the eligibility criteria prescribed by APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU) and the Government of Kerala are eligible for admission to the MCA programme.

The MCET Advantage

The MCA programme at MCET is designed to create industry-ready professionals through a combination of quality education, practical learning, and professional skill development.

Add-on Certifications

To complement the university curriculum, MCET offers specialized add-on courses that equip students with practical knowledge of industry-leading technologies and tools.

Career & Placement

The Career Guidance and Placement Cell (CGPC) functions as a dedicated platform for enhancing students' employability through continuous training, industry interaction, and campus recruitment support.
