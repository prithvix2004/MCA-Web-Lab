Mohandas College of Engineering and Technology

MCA Association - MASTEC

Department of Computer Applications

The MCA regular program started functioning in the year 2005 with a sanctioned strength of sixty students. The program remains committed towards its goal of producing not only industry ready professionals, but also to provide the right ambience and infrastructure for those interested in pursuing their interest in computers and related fields. Master of Computer Applications program is a two year course of intensive theory and practical work. It is spread over a period of six semesters which enables the candidates to get familiar with the usage of computer using at least five latest programming languages and a large number of applications.

Expert faculty drawn from industry and academia are the catalysts to the learning on campus and facilitate valuable exposure to the learning on campus and provide an integrated multidisciplinary program with innovative teaching methods to enhance analytic and problem solving skills, personality development module to tap the full potential of the students by improving time and self management communication skills, team spirit, confidence and career building aptitude.

MASTEC is a forum of students and staff of MCA, which strives at the total personality development of students. It is a platform that helps the students of MCA to grow with technical skills and also helps in the overall development of the student. It is actively involved in conducting invited lectures, seminars, webinars, cultural and technical events for the students.
