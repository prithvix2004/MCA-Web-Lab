Department of Computer Applications

Mohandas College of Engineering and Technology (MCET)

Innovate. Code. Lead the Digital Future.

The Department of Computer Applications at MCET offers a four-year Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) programme affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU), Kerala. The curriculum covers AI, Machine Learning, Data Science, Cloud Computing, and Cybersecurity.

Programme Quick Facts

Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters)
Mode: Full-time Programme
Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, & Math (min. 50%)
Intake: 120 Seats
Affiliation: AICTE Approved & KTU Affiliated
